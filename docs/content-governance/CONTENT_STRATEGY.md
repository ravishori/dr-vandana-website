# Psychology Content Strategy

## Purpose

Build a coherent, trustworthy and discoverable psychology learning library for Dr. Vandana Rajiv Chaudhary. Every content series should have a distinct reader promise, while the master registry prevents accidental repetition across sections and channels.

## Audience

General readers seeking accessible psychology education, everyday coping strategies, relationship and behaviour insights, and guidance on when professional support may help. Content is educational and must not be presented as an individual diagnosis or a substitute for assessment or treatment.

## Editorial principles

1. **Science + humanity + clarity + practical value.** Explain concepts accurately in natural language and treat readers respectfully.
2. **Evidence before claims.** Prefer peer-reviewed reviews and primary studies where appropriate, established theories, and recognised psychological or medical institutions. Verify current statistics and public-programme claims against authoritative sources.
3. **No fabricated evidence.** Never invent research, quotations, statistics, credentials, citations or clinical conclusions. Distinguish established evidence, emerging findings and professional interpretation.
4. **Non-stigmatising language.** Avoid fear, blame, sensationalism, pop-psychology labels and diagnosing a person from a general description.
5. **Practical but bounded advice.** Offer realistic strategies; explain limitations and recommend qualified help when relevant without unnecessary alarm.
6. **Distinct editorial purpose.** A shared concept does not make two articles duplicates, but a new title alone does not make a genuinely new topic.
7. **Human approval.** Dr. Vandana or an explicitly authorised reviewer must approve psychological claims and final copy before publication. AI assistance does not replace review.
8. **Platform adaptation.** Maintain one accurate core message, then adapt structure and length for Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram and the website. Do not blindly copy a long article into every channel.
9. **Privacy and professional boundaries.** Do not publish identifiable client information or imply a therapeutic relationship with social-media readers.
10. **Traceability.** Every item must have a registry ID, status, section, core question, review state and evidence for publication claims.

## Content lifecycle

**Discover → registry search → overlap assessment → reserve → research → draft → scientific/editorial review → approve → publish → verify → archive/update.**

A topic may not enter drafting until its existing-topic and semantic-overlap check is recorded. Reservation does not equal approval of the eventual article. A draft does not equal a published post.

## Evidence record

For each substantive article, retain a working evidence list with source title, author/organisation, publication date, URL/DOI, the claim supported, and any limitations. Prioritise systematic reviews/meta-analyses and authoritative clinical guidance when appropriate; use original studies to explain specific findings. Recheck changing statistics, government initiatives and current guidance before publication.

## Measurement

Where data is available, review reach, meaningful engagement, saves/shares, outbound clicks and reader questions. Use performance to improve presentation and accessibility—not to justify exaggerated claims or sensational topics. Record metrics separately from scientific approval.

## Content ownership

- **Editorial owner:** to be assigned/confirmed.
- **Psychological/scientific reviewer:** Dr. Vandana Rajiv Chaudhary or authorised qualified reviewer; confirm per item.
- **Publisher/operator:** to be assigned/confirmed.
- **Publication evidence:** actual public URL, platform post ID or an auditable record; never inferred.

## Scope and implementation status

This strategy establishes editorial governance only. It does not add application code, automate deduplication, schedule posts or confirm that a newsletter or channel integration is live. Engineering changes require a separate approved task and must respect the repository's existing architecture and safety boundaries.
