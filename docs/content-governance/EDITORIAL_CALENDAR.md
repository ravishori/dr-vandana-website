# Editorial Calendar

This calendar is a planning aid. It must distinguish ideas from approved plans, scheduled posts from verified publications, and series-level plans from individual article topics.

## Status rules

Use the status vocabulary in `README.md`. A date belongs here only when it is supported by a source or an explicit editorial decision. Record the source of a schedule when available. Do not infer publication from a scheduled date.

## Weekly planning workflow

1. Review the upcoming calendar and any outstanding reservations.
2. Confirm the topic ID, section and overlap decision.
3. Confirm evidence and reviewer availability.
4. Agree the target date and intended channels; record the source of the decision.
5. Draft platform-specific versions and assets.
6. Obtain approval before scheduling/publishing.
7. Record the scheduled state separately from published state.
8. Verify each live post and add its actual date, URL/post ID and relevant notes.
9. Review conflicts and update the calendar and registry together.

## Calendar integrity

- Do not replace `Unknown` with an estimate.
- Do not treat a campaign date range as proof that each daily post exists.
- Do not mark a post `published` based only on a calendar entry or successful scheduling request.
- Keep canceled or retired topics in the registry for future deduplication.
