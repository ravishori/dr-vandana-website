# Newsletter Strategy — The Psychology Compass

## Status

**Proposed editorial series.** The inspected website repository does not establish that a newsletter publishing feature, email subscription system or automated social distribution is implemented. This document is a plan, not an implementation or launch announcement.

## Editorial promise

*The Psychology Compass* is a proposed weekly, evidence-informed psychology feature by Dr. Vandana Rajiv Chaudhary, designed to help readers understand one psychological question and leave with a useful, realistic takeaway.

**Proposed identity**
- Name: The Psychology Compass
- Descriptor: Weekly Psychology Insights
- Professional identity: Dr. Vandana Rajiv Chaudhary — Psychologist | 6 Years of Professional Experience
- Tagline: Heal Your Mind, Body & Soul
- Frequency: Weekly, subject to editorial approval and capacity
- First publication date: Unknown
- Issue 1 topic: Not selected

## Issue format

1. Issue number and date (only when confirmed).
2. Clear title built around one reader question.
3. Relatable opening without fear or sensationalism.
4. Plain-language psychological explanation.
5. Everyday example and practical strategies.
6. Evidence note and source list appropriate to the topic.
7. Boundaries/when to seek qualified support, where relevant.
8. Warm conclusion and a clear next step.
9. Link to the canonical website article when available.
10. Professionally approved assets and platform-specific versions.

Each issue must have a registry ID. If it expands or curates an existing blog, record the source article ID and explain whether it is a variant or a genuinely distinct article.

## Distribution options

### Stage 1 — Manual social series

Use the existing editorial workflow and available native platform tools. Publish only after approval. Verify scheduling and permissions for each platform at the time of setup.

### Stage 2 — Website article archive and social distribution

Publish the canonical article on the website, then create distinct Facebook, WhatsApp and Telegram variants through the separate social publisher where the supported integrations and permissions have been verified. Keep the article as the source of truth and log each channel's status separately.

The social publisher's repository documents Telegram support, Facebook API-dependent publishing and WhatsApp as an official-API placeholder pending verification. Do not claim WhatsApp publishing is implemented or available until that capability and account configuration are confirmed.

### Stage 3 — Optional email newsletter

Add an opt-in subscriber experience only if approved as a separate feature. Requirements include:
- explicit consent and a clear description of email frequency/purpose;
- secure subscriber storage and restricted access;
- confirmation/verification where appropriate;
- unsubscribe and suppression handling;
- privacy notice and retention policy;
- delivery, bounce and unsubscribe monitoring;
- secure provider credentials and auditable operational controls.

Facebook followers are not automatically email subscribers. A Facebook post or scheduled Page post is not itself an email newsletter.

## Editorial and safety gates

Before each issue is approved:
- check the master registry and semantic-overlap rules;
- verify claims against appropriate evidence and record sources;
- avoid diagnosis, stigma, unsupported causation and guaranteed outcomes;
- distinguish general education from individual psychological treatment;
- obtain Dr. Vandana's or an authorised qualified reviewer's approval;
- confirm the final copy, image and channel variants;
- verify links, publication permissions and scheduling configuration.

No fully automatic publication of unreviewed psychological content.

## Operational record

Track at minimum:
- issue ID and registry topic ID;
- working title and status;
- evidence/review state;
- target date and actual publication date;
- canonical article URL;
- per-channel variant, scheduled/published state and platform URL/post ID;
- asset references and approvals;
- errors/retries where automated distribution is later implemented.
